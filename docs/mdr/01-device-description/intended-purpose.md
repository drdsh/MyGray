## Intended purpose
MyGray is intended to collect, store, analyse and display radiation dose
information generated by X-ray based imaging modalities and nuclear medicine,
to support medical physics experts and radiology staff in monitoring and
optimising patient exposure, comparing with diagnostic reference levels and
identifying exposures requiring follow-up.

## Contraindications, limitations, warnings
- Not intended for real-time guidance during a procedure.
- Dose values are as reported by the modality (RDSR); MyGray does not measure dose.
- Absence of an alert does not imply absence of risk.
