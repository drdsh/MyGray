# MG-PMS-001 Post-Market Surveillance Plan (MDR Art. 84, Annex III)
- Sources: complaints, service tickets, field telemetry (opt-in, no PHI: alert-code statistics, quarantine rates per vendor/model), vulnerability feeds (cargo-deny advisories, CVE), literature, similar device recalls.
- Indicators & thresholds: quarantine rate per device model > x % → investigation; any DCM-0001/0005/0006 in field → CAPA review.
- PSUR (Class IIa: at least every 2 years, Art. 86), trend reporting (Art. 88), vigilance (Art. 87, MIR form), FSCA.
- PMCF plan or justification for not performing PMCF.
